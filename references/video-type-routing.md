# Video type routing

Use presets as analytical hints, not as rigid templates. A video may combine several types.

## Common families

- UGC selfie or talking head
- Influencer recommendation
- Unboxing or review
- Product demonstration or tutorial
- Before/after transformation
- Problem-solution narrative
- Micro-drama, comedy, or reversal
- Testimonial or interview
- Cinematic product film
- Music-synced montage or trailer
- ASMR or food preparation
- Beauty and skincare
- Fashion try-on
- Home or furniture transformation
- Consumer electronics showcase
- App or screen-recording demo
- Livestream highlight
- Vlog or documentary
- Animation, stop-motion, or VFX
- Virtual presenter or lip-sync

## Generalization vector

Always score these dimensions even when the family is unknown:

- Human presence and identity dependence
- Dialogue and lip-sync dependence
- Product interaction dependence
- Transformation dependence
- Audio-beat dependence
- Shot density
- Scene count
- Camera complexity
- Text/UI dependence
- Continuity dependence
- Source duration and target compression pressure

Use the vector to change emphasis. A music montage prioritizes cut and beat anchors; a transformation video prioritizes state endpoints and continuity; a product film prioritizes visual identity, camera, composition, materials, and lighting; talking-head content prioritizes identity, speech timing, expression, and product handling.

Unknown formats must still route through the vector instead of falling back to a generic prompt.
